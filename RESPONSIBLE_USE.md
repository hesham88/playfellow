# Responsible use

PlayFellow is research on game-playing AI that sees a screen and presses keys, built to play **with** people: a co-op partner, an accessibility aid, a way to learn how embodied AI works.

## Our position
- **Non-violence. No war.** This technology must never be used for military or warfare purposes, weapons, targeting, or violence against people. Software that perceives a screen and acts on it is a general capability; we choose where ours goes.
- **No surveillance.** Not for watching, tracking or profiling people without their informed consent, and not for espionage.
- **Fair play.** Not for cheating against other players, and not for automating online or multiplayer games against their terms.
- **Adults only.** PlayFellow products are for people aged 18 and over.

The binding terms are in the [LICENSE](LICENSE).

## Safety built into the code
- **F12**, or moving the mouse to the top-left corner, stops everything at once and releases held keys.
- Keys go only to the one game window you chose, and only while it is in front.
- Every action is checked against that game's control card; anything else is dropped.
- Launchers, stores, browsers and payment windows are never controlled.
- Time and AI-call limits end a session.
