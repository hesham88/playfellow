# Contributing

This repository is maintained by a single author, [@hesham88](https://github.com/hesham88), and **does not accept pull requests**.

You are welcome to:
- open an **issue** to report a bug, ask a question or suggest an idea;
- **fork** it and build on it under the terms of the [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

Ideas from issues may be implemented by the maintainer; by opening an issue you agree that what you write there is "Not a Contribution" in the sense of the licence unless you say otherwise.
